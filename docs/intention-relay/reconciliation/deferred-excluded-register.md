# Deferred and Excluded Register

This register is the sole coordination index for claims intentionally outside
current implementation scope. It does not authorize future work. Every item has
a disposition and a condition for reconsideration; “as recorded in concept” is
not sufficient evidence.

## Delivery owners named by the roadmap

The roadmap's Milestone 5+ section and the Mandate-track milestones now name the
delivery owner for the adopted rows; each activation still needs its own
approved specification:

- Milestone 10 (Mandate core) delivers EXC-011 (calendar/interval/time-zone/DST
  semantics);
- Milestone 11 (Mandate execution plane) delivers EXC-010 (worker/process
  supervision topology) together with the Mandate tool-loop, child, and bridge
  implementations;
- Milestone 12 (capabilities and context) delivers EXC-008 (rich MIME/raw kernel
  output projection) together with the MCP and kernel implementations;
- the Slice 3 activation delivers EXC-041 (autonomous harness goal mode) and
  EXC-042 (work/requeue after client disconnection), together with the
  tool-descriptor, tool-registry, model-tool-loop, bridge-invocation, and
  MCP-method-catalog contract records (activated by
  [ADR 0044](../decisions/0044-m5plus-slice3-harness.md));
- the Slice 4 activation delivers EXC-009 (physical deletion/GC retention
  policy), EXC-015 (activity numeric limit classification), EXC-039 (export),
  EXC-040 (cross-workspace clone/rebind), and EXC-043 (consolidated RLM package
  boundary);
- EXC-044 is the M5+ five-slice sequence itself: Slice 1 (contracts/versions) and
  Slice 2 (control plane) are delivered, and the Slice 3, Slice 4, and Slice 5
  activations carry the rest;
- the fifth Milestone 5+ slice (instruction sources and system context)
  delivers the instruction channel of
  [ADR 0043](../decisions/0043-instruction-sources-and-system-context.md); its
  deferrals (EXC-059..065) stay non-authorizing with their own reconsideration
  owners;
- the remaining adopted rows (EXC-021..023, EXC-025..030, EXC-037, EXC-038) are
  owned by their Milestone 5+ detail packages, whose exit criteria name the
  activation.
