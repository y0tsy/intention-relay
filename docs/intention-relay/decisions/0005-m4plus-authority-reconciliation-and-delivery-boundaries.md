# 0005: M4+ Authority Reconciliation and Delivery Boundaries

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

M4+ opens with a documentation-only authority reconciliation package that authorizes no implementation on its own: it
coordinates later work, and its reconciliation registers are retired. Closed M4 remains a historical baseline. M5-M9 are
not renumbered or silently replaced in this package. Later roadmap changes require separately approved owner documents,
decision records, quality declarations, test-first evidence, and implementation specifications.

## Non-goals

No source, Cargo, quality-policy activation, Makefile, CI, migration, protocol, crate activation, or M4 charter/closeout
change is authorized here.

Evidence: complete topic coverage, one owner per Foundation rule, explicit later deferrals, and consistent authoritative
references, with the existing documentation/architecture/quality gate evidence.

Provenance: `m4plus_concept.md`, recommendation, verification portfolio, and preparation checklist sections.
