# 0005: M4+ Authority Reconciliation and Delivery Boundaries

## Status

Accepted.

## Decision

M4+ begins with a documentation-only authority reconciliation package. The
reconciliation matrix, compatibility register, contradiction register,
ownership/dependency map, and concept supersession index coordinate later
work but never authorize implementation on their own.

Closed M4 remains a historical baseline. M5-M9 are not renumbered or silently
replaced in this package. Later roadmap changes require separately approved
owner documents, decision records, quality declarations, test-first evidence,
and implementation specifications.

## Non-goals

No source, Cargo, quality-policy activation, Makefile, CI, migration, protocol,
crate activation, or M4 charter/closeout change is authorized here.

## Evidence

The package exits only with complete topic coverage, one owner per Foundation
rule, explicit later deferrals, consistent authoritative references, and the
existing documentation/architecture/quality gate evidence.

## Provenance

`m4plus_concept.md`, recommendation, verification portfolio, and preparation
checklist sections.
